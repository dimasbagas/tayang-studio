Privacy Policy

Tayang Studio · Effective 25 November 2026

Tayang Studio is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. It has no user account on a Tayang server and does not upload your media anywhere except the platform you explicitly post to. This policy explains the limited data the app handles.

1. Data kept on your device

DataWhere it livesWhy
Projects, renders, transcripts, settingsYour chosen working folder and the local app databaseTo edit and render your videos
YouTube / Facebook / TikTok account ids, names, and avatarsLocal app databaseTo show which accounts you can post to
OAuth client secrets and access/refresh tokensWindows Credential ManagerTo upload on your behalf without asking you to sign in again

Tokens are never written to logs, analytics, or any server operated by the author.

2. TikTok data

When you connect TikTok, TikTok's authorisation screen asks you to approve user.info.basic and video.publish. With your approval the app receives:

  • your TikTok open_id and basic profile (display name, username, avatar);
  • an access token and a refresh token, with their expiry times.

This information is used only to show your connected account and to upload the videos you select through TikTok's Content Posting API. Videos are sent from your computer directly to TikTok's servers. The app does not read your TikTok feed, messages, followers, or analytics, and does not post anything you did not ask it to post.

TikTok's handling of data after upload is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

3. Analytics (optional)

If you turn on Share anonymous stats in Settings, the app sends anonymous product events to PostHog: which feature was used (for example "video rendered" or "post made"), the app version, operating system, and UI language. It never sends video, audio, prompts, chat text, file names, or account tokens. The setting is off until you enable it and can be turned off at any time.

4. What the app does not do

  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • No advertising or tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No reading of your files beyond the folders you choose.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data stays until you delete it. Disconnecting an account in Settings → Account deletes its tokens from Windows Credential Manager and its entry from the local database. Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes the rest. To request deletion of analytics data, open an issue with your install id (shown in Settings).

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and should not be used by them.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: open an issue.
