Long videos in. Short clips out. Posted where you publish.

Tayang Studio is a Windows desktop app for creators. It finds the best moments in a long video, cuts them into captioned vertical clips, and uploads them to your own YouTube channel, Facebook Page, or TikTok profile.

See how it works

Windows 10 and 11 · Your files stay on your computer

What it does

One workspace from raw recording to a published clip.

Autoclip

Paste a YouTube link and get 58–120 second vertical clips with burned-in captions, framed for Shorts, Reels, and TikTok.

Autopilot

Describe a series once. Tayang writes, renders, and schedules each episode, then posts it on the days you picked.

Post everywhere

Connect your own accounts with each platform's official login. Multi-account, per-post privacy, and no re-uploading by hand.

How it works

The app renders locally, then uploads through the platform's official API.

  1. Add your source. A long recording, a project, or a YouTube link.
  2. Pick the moments. The built-in agent proposes hooks and cuts; you approve the list.
  3. Render. Captions, watermark, layout, and loudness are handled on your machine.
  4. Publish. Choose accounts and privacy, then post now or schedule it.

Your accounts, your rules

Tayang Studio never asks for a platform password. Each connection uses the platform's own authorisation screen, and tokens are stored in Windows Credential Manager on your PC.

YouTube

Upload as a Short or a standard 16:9 video, to one or many channels you manage.

Facebook

Publish to Pages as a Reel when vertical, or as a normal Page video.

TikTok

Direct Post through the Content Posting API, with the privacy level you choose.

Questions

Does my video leave my computer?

Only when you post it. Rendering and clipping happen locally. The finished file is uploaded straight to the platform you selected.

Do I need a Tayang account?

No. The free posting routes use your own platform apps and accounts. Optional cloud features are separate.

Can I manage more than one account?

Yes. Connect as many YouTube channels, Facebook Pages, or TikTok profiles as you are allowed to manage, and pick per post.

What does the app store about me?

Account names and tokens on your device, and nothing else unless you turn on anonymous analytics. See the Privacy Policy.
