What Tayang Studio is

Tayang Studio is a desktop application that helps a creator turn long videos into short vertical clips, render them on the creator's own computer, and — when the creator connects an account — upload the result to that creator's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page, or TikTok profile.

TikTok integration

The app uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. A creator signs in on TikTok's own website, approves the user.info.basic and video.publish scopes, and the app then uploads only the videos the creator explicitly selects. There is no automatic posting without the creator's action or an autopilot schedule they configured themselves.
